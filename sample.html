Chairperson

Select the overall head of the student body. The Chairperson leads the student council and represents the students' voice.

Vice Chairperson

Assists the Chairperson and steps in when needed. The Vice Chairperson is a key supporting role.

General Secretary

Responsible for the administration of the student council, managing records, and coordinating activities across all departments.

University Union Councillor

Represents the college at the university level. The UUC ensures the college's interests are upheld in university matters.

Lady Representatives

Two dedicated representatives for female students. They address specific concerns and promote women's welfare on campus.

Magazine Editor

Curates the college magazine, encouraging creative writing and showcasing student talent.

Arts Secretary

Organizes cultural events and competitions, fostering a vibrant arts community within the college.

Sports Secretary

Coordinates sports activities and tournaments, promoting physical fitness and team spirit among students.

Class Representatives

Represent individual classes to the faculty and student council. CRs act as a bridge for communication.
